Investigating Cross-Lingual Transfer Learning for Urdu Text Using Word Embeddings

ICOSST 2021

Shujah Ur Rehman

This paper investigates cross-lingual transfer learning methods for Urdu text classification tasks. The work explores word embedding-based approaches and evaluates their effectiveness on various NLP tasks including propaganda detection, topic classification, and sentiment analysis.
